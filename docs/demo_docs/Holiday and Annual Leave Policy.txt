HOLIDAY AND ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY
All staff. Effective 1 January.

1. ENTITLEMENT
All full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
in addition to public holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement
based on contracted hours.

2. CARRYOVER
Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the following calendar year. Carried-over
days must be taken by 31 March or they are forfeited. Carryover beyond 5 days requires
written approval from your line manager.

3. BOOKING NOTICE
Leave must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance for absences of 5 days or fewer,
and at least 4 weeks in advance for longer absences. Requests are approved by your
line manager subject to team coverage.

4. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
Public holidays are granted in addition to the 25-day entitlement. Staff required to
work a public holiday receive a day in lieu.

5. SICKNESS DURING LEAVE
If you fall ill during booked annual leave, those days may be reclassified as sick
leave on production of a medical certificate.

6. LEAVERS
On termination, accrued but untaken leave is paid in lieu. Leave taken in excess of
accrual is deducted from the final salary payment.
