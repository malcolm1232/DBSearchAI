PROJECT FALCON — CONFIDENTIAL PLAN
Deal team only. Do not distribute.

1. OVERVIEW
Project Falcon is the codename for the proposed acquisition of Falcon Analytics Ltd,
a Bristol-based real-time data pipeline vendor. The transaction is structured as a
cash-and-stock purchase with an anticipated close in Q3.

2. VALUATION
Headline enterprise value is $4.2 billion, comprising $3.1 billion in cash and
$1.1 billion in stock. The implied multiple is 11.4x forward revenue. An additional
earn-out of up to $300 million is payable against 2027 ARR milestones.

3. BUDGET AND MILESTONES
Integration budget is $250,000 for the first phase.
  - Weeks 1-3:   diligence close-out and technical architecture review
  - Weeks 4-9:   data-plane migration onto the shared analytics pipeline
  - Weeks 10-12: customer migration, contract novation, and go-live

4. DEAL TEAM
Corporate development lead, external counsel, and the assigned integration architect.
Access to this document is restricted to the deal team. Wider distribution requires
sign-off from the transaction committee.

5. RISKS
Key-person retention at Falcon Analytics is the principal execution risk. A retention
pool of $18 million has been reserved and is not included in the headline valuation.
